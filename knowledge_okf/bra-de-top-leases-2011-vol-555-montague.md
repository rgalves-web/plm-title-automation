## Bra-De Oil & Gas: as leases do Vol. 555 (jan/2011) sao TOP LEASES, nao ratificacoes

Em Montague, quando uma lease EOG com origem Bra-De aparece com recording no **Vol. 555** e data
**11/01/2011** (ou proxima), o instrumento e uma **lease nova em regime de top lease**, nao uma
ratificacao nem uma cessao. A linguagem que decide esta no *Memorandum of Oil, Gas and Mineral
Lease*: "the lands covered hereby are also covered by prior oil and gas lease (the 'Prior Lease')
dated March 31, 2008, recorded in Volume 441, Page 275 ... The parties hereof agree that the term
of this Lease shall commence and the estate created hereby shall vest when said Prior Lease
terminates, but in no event ... after the expiration of three (3) years from the date of this
Lease."

**Consequencia pratica para datas:** o EXP do Lease & Mineral Schedule nao conta 3 anos da data do
instrumento, e sim do vencimento da Prior Lease. Ex.: top lease de 11/01/2011 sobre prior lease de
31/03/2008 (3 anos) -> vigencia inicia 31/03/2011 -> **EXP 2014-03-30**, EXT 2016-03-30. Bate com o
Schedule.

**Cadeia ate a EOG.** Assignment of Oil, Gas and Mineral Leases, efetivo **31/01/2011**, file
**113905, Vol 563, Pgs 849-851** (K1401501), Bra-De Oil and Gas, Inc. -> EOG Resources, Inc. O
**Exhibit "A" (p.3) lista lease a lease com o recording do memorando** — e a prova de cobertura que
faltava na ficha `cadeia-bra-de-oil-and-gas-para-eog-montague`. Conteudo lido: Paul W. Hill
(555/471), James A. Holloway (555/437), Dale Harrison (555/474), Loetta Sossaman (555/460), Jan
Irons (555/465), Dorothy McKeen (555/458).

Reserva de ORRI identica a das outras cessoes Bra-De, **com extensao expressa a "new lease,
renewal(s), extension(s), or top lease(s)"** tomados ate 1 ano apos surrender/termino, e piso
"Assignee's net revenue interests will not be less than 75%".

O assignment **de 2008** (Vol 452/821, 15/08/2008) cobre as leases de 2008 (Leases 1 e 2 do TO
000-363) e e **limitado aos depths abaixo do topo do Barnett Shale**.
